Choosing the right AI: ML or deep learning?

A case analysis of two real-world AI systems, and why one problem calls for traditional machine learning while the other needs deep learning.

Overview

Match the tool to the problem

Picking the wrong model wastes time, data, and computing power. For this project, I chose one simple machine learning example and one deep learning example from my coursework, found a real-world application of each, and explained why each approach fits its problem and why the other one doesn't.

The two cases

One table, one image

Machine learning

Customer churn prediction

Model
Support vector machine (SVM)
Real world
A subscription service predicts which customers will cancel and offers them deals first.
Why ML fits
Clean table of known features, moderate data size, and managers need to see why customers leave.
Deep learning

Medical image recognition

Model
Convolutional neural network (CNN)
Real world
Detects diabetic eye disease from retinal photos at specialist-level accuracy. The FDA cleared a similar AI screening tool in 2018.
Why DL fits
Raw image input, a large labeled dataset, and accuracy matters most.
Decision framework

Four questions that decide it

QuestionChoose MLChoose DL
What does the data look like?Structured tables with known featuresRaw images, audio, or text
How much data is there?Thousands of recordsHundreds of thousands of labeled examples
Must decisions be explained?Yes, people need the reasonsAccuracy matters more than reasons
Computing budget?A normal computerGPUs for training
Challenges and solutions

What made it hard

01

Credible evidence

Many online examples are vague marketing claims. I used a peer-reviewed churn study, a peer-reviewed JAMA study, and an FDA announcement instead.

02

Why the other fails

Saying why a method works is easy. Showing why the alternative is a poor fit took reasoning about data type, data size, explainability, and cost.

03

Clear for any reader

I built the report around a side-by-side comparison so a non-technical reader can follow the decision.
